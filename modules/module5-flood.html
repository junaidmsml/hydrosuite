Gumbel & Log-Pearson III Frequency Analysis

Fit an extreme value distribution to annual maximum rainfall, read design depths for any return period with 95% limits, and check the fit before using the numbers.

What This Module Does

Return Period Analysis

Design rainfall for any return period you list, from 2 up to 10,000 years.

Three Methods

Gumbel (method of moments), Gumbel with Yn and Sn for your sample size, and Log-Pearson Type III.

Goodness of Fit

Kolmogorov-Smirnov test, RMSE and the fitted return period of your biggest event.

Seven Graphs

Frequency curve with 95% band, design rainfall bars, annual maxima vs design levels, histogram with fitted PDF, CDF with K-S band, Q-Q plot and a three-method comparison.

Excel Export

Download every graph and all result tables in one Excel workbook, or the graphs alone as a PNG ZIP.

Design Flood Estimation — Input

Data needed: one annual maximum (highest daily rainfall of the year) per year, as year, max_rainfall. At least 10 years are required and 30 or more are recommended. Values are depths in mm, so the output is design rainfall, not discharge.
