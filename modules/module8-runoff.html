SCS Curve Number & Rational Method

Convert storm rainfall into direct runoff with the SCS Curve Number method (with antecedent moisture), check it against observed runoff, and estimate a peak flow with the Rational Method.

What This Module Does

SCS Curve Number

Q = (P − Ia)² / (P − Ia + S) for each storm, with AMC I, II or III and an adjustable Ia/S ratio.

Rational Method

Qp = C·i·A / 3.6 for small catchments, plus a peak for every storm from its depth and duration.

Validation

Add observed runoff to get NSE, RMSE, bias and a least-squares best-fit CN.

Six Charts

Event bars, scatter, rainfall partition, runoff classes, cumulative volumes and CN curves for all three AMC levels.

Runoff Estimation — Input

Data needed: one row per storm event: label, rainfall_mm and optionally observed_runoff_mm. The label can be a year (for example the annual maximum daily rainfall) or a date. The SCS-CN method works on single storms, so do not enter annual rainfall totals.
