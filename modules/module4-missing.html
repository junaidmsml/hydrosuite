Estimate Missing Rainfall Using Nearby Stations

Fill gaps in a rainfall record with the Normal Ratio Method, then test how good the method is on your own data before trusting the filled values.

What This Module Does

Gap Detection

Empty cells, NaN, NA or negative codes in the target column are treated as missing.

Normal Ratio Method

Px = (1/n) Σ (Nx/Ni) × Pi, with normals taken over the same years for both stations.

Station Selection

Use the best correlated neighbours, column order, or pick stations yourself (1 to 5).

Validation

Leave-one-out test on known years gives RMSE, MAE, bias and R² for the estimates.
