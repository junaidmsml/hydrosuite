Linear Trend + Prediction Intervals

Project future rainfall for the next 5–50 years using linear regression on historical data. Includes prediction intervals, a slope significance test, and multi-chart visualization for water resources planning and climate adaptation.

What This Module Does

Linear Trend Extrapolation

Fits a linear model and projects rainfall forward for user-defined horizon.

Confidence Bands

Provides prediction intervals (80–99%) showing the range of uncertainty.

Multi-Decade Forecast

Project rainfall decade-by-decade with cumulative totals.

Rich Visualization

Bar charts, area charts, pie charts, and combined trend plots.
