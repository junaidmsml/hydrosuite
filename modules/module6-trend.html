Mann-Kendall Trend Test & Sen's Slope Estimator

Detect statistically significant trends in your rainfall record using the non-parametric Mann-Kendall test — the standard method in climate science for identifying monotonic trends without assuming any distribution.

What This Module Does

Mann-Kendall Test

Non-parametric test for monotonic trends — no assumption of normal distribution.

Sen's Slope

Robust estimate of trend magnitude (mm/year) with 95% confidence interval.

Statistical Significance

Z-statistic and p-value to determine if the trend is statistically significant.

Trend Visualization

Time series plot with fitted trend line and confidence band overlay.
