Isohyetal Method for Hilly & Varied Terrain

Build a rainfall surface from your stations, draw true isohyets, and compute the basin average from the area between them. The result is checked against Thiessen, the surface mean and the arithmetic mean.

What This Module Does

True Isohyets

Contour lines traced with marching squares on an IDW surface, on a map with coordinates, scale bar and basin outline.

Isohyetal Average

P̄ = Σ[(Pᵢ+Pᵢ₊₁)/2 × Aᵢ] / ΣAᵢ over the basin, with a table of band areas in km².

Method Comparison

Isohyetal vs Thiessen vs IDW surface mean vs arithmetic mean, with basin rainfall volume.

Check and Export

Leave-one-out test of the interpolation, then Excel, PNG, ZIP and GIS-ready grid downloads.

Contour-Based Rainfall — Input

Data needed: rainfall (mm) for the same period at each station, with latitude and longitude in decimal degrees. At least 3 stations are needed and 5 or more are recommended, because isohyets drawn from very few gauges are rough.
